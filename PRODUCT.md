# AGV Phase 01

## Register

brand

## Users

The project owner reviewing an unofficial motorcycle helmet portfolio concept in Chrome, on mobile and desktop.

## Product Purpose

A browser-previewable structural reconstruction of the seven regions documented in AGV_SELF_CONTAINED_WIREFRAME_CODEX.md. That brief is the single source of truth; this file is only a context summary.

## Brand Personality

Precise, focused, racing-inspired. Futuristic / Neo-Racing is reserved for a later visual phase.

## Anti-references

Generic motorcycle storefronts, full-dark landing pages, neon dashboards, stock photography, generated helmet imagery, checkout flows, and unrelated content sections.

## Design Principles

- Reproduce the documented geometry and responsive composition.
- Keep media as static, labeled grayscale placeholders.
- Make navigation, gallery, FAQ and section links work.
- Preserve both original development briefs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable controls, modal focus management, readable contrast, reduced motion, and usable touch targets. No unverified helmet safety claims.
